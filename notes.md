# Milestone 1: Community and first reading

**Community:** r/soccer (https://old.reddit.com/r/soccer), public, no login
needed to read. It's a general football community, not built around one show
or release.

**What I read:** comments from post-match threads, match threads, a stats post
and a transfer-news thread.

## Candidate distinctions

1. **Some comments cite a number or checkable fact, most don't.** A few quote
   goals, xG, a score, a transfer fee or a date. Most make their point with no
   evidence at all.
2. **Some judge a person, others just express a feeling.** "He's finished at
   this level" is a judgement about a player. "I can't watch this anymore" is
   only how the writer feels right now.
3. **Some are written in the moment, others are written after the fact.**
   Match-thread comments react to the last few minutes. Comments on stats and
   transfer posts look back over a season or a career.
4. **A lot of comments are jokes or sarcasm.** They often hide a real opinion
   ("yeah he's definitely worth £100m"), which makes them hard to sort.
